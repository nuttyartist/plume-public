# Plume is now Daino Notes

Daino Notes website: https://www.get-notes.com

We rebranded to Daino Notes as part of our vision to develop a suite of beautiful and intuitive digital tools that help you capture and organize your thoughts. By unifying our apps under the [Daino](https://daino.io?utm_source=plume_github) brand, we aim to provide a cohesive and integrated experience across all our products.

## What will happen to all my notes?

Rest assured, all your notes and data are completely safe and intact. The rebranding to Daino Notes has not affected your existing data in any way.
